Please note the following important information:
This case primarily concerns the amendment of real-name registration details. The government requires that online games in China be subject to real-name authentication. However, foreign platforms such as Steam are still not required to implement real-name authentication.
The court assists the heir in amending the real-name registration details with the game company, thereby effectively enabling the inheritance of the account; however, if the account itself does not require real-name authentication, the situation may differ from that in this case.
